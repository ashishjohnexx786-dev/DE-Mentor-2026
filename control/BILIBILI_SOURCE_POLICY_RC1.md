# Bilibili Source Policy — RC1

Bilibili is allowed as a discovery and secondary teaching source, but it is not automatically a primary dependency.

## Keep / use when
- The spoken teaching is English (or otherwise usable by the learner).
- The video materially teaches the exact competency better than the available primary source.
- The upload appears legitimate or is clearly mirroring openly available content with an identifiable original.
- Learner access works on the real device.
- A fallback source is retained when long-term availability is uncertain.

## Reject as permanent primary source when
- It is an unauthorized re-upload of a paid Udemy/Coursera/ZeroToMastery/etc. course.
- Ownership/source cannot be established.
- The page cannot be reliably checked and there is no fallback.
- Chinese navigation makes the lesson materially harder and an equally good original source exists.

## Final-audit rule
Every retained Bilibili link must pass: open on learner phone -> correct title/content -> English teaching usable -> exact topic match -> legitimacy check -> fallback check. If the same material exists on the original creator/vendor/YouTube page, the original is preferred.
