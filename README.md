# Standalone Data Engineer 2026 — Mentor edition 1.2

Live course: https://ashishjohnexx786-dev.github.io/DE-Mentor-2026/

A complete beginner route: M00–M19, 264 lessons, 50 project phases and seven assessment gates. Start with **How to study**, then DE00-01. The mentor includes the complete lesson text, instructor video assignments, PDFs, practical downloads and optional browser-local progress.

## Study flow

Watch the assigned instructor source, understand the written example, do the practical task, save your attempt and review it. At each gate follow A → Review A → different fresh B → Review B. The mentor records your study decisions; it does not automatically grade your answers.

AMOLED and light themes, adjustable reading size and an optional stopwatch are available in Settings. Export progress to transfer between devices; phone and PC do not synchronize automatically. Videos require access to their original provider. Visited lessons and PDFs may be cached by the browser; large ZIP downloads use the network.

Download the complete course from Settings. Read VERIFICATION.md for the checks and native-runtime limits. No generated videos are included.

## Repository layout

- index.html, styles.css, app.js: mentor interface.
- curriculum.json and data/: the single current course edition.
- materials/: canonical learner, revision, test and protected-review PDFs.
- practice/: module and gate practice packages, including their setup instructions.
- downloads/: full cumulative course ZIP.
- sw.js and manifest.webmanifest: scoped offline caching and app metadata.
- release.json: public edition marker.
